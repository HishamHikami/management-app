In the app (immediate)

Open the app → tap your picture at the top right → Profile → Delete my account, and confirm with your password.

Without the app

{% if contact %}Email {{ contact }} or ask{% else %}Ask{% endif %} an admin of {{ org_name }} to delete your account. It is done within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, phone number, email, position and password.
  • Your committee memberships, notifications, notification tokens, invitations and replies to upcoming meetings. You are signed out on every phone.

What is kept

Tasks, comments, announcements and meeting minutes you wrote stay for the association's records, shown as written by "Former member". Server backups containing your old data are erased automatically after 14 days.
